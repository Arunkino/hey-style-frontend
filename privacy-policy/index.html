1. Introduction

Welcome to HeyStyle ("HeyStyle", "we", "our", or "us").

HeyStyle is a digital platform designed to connect customers with salons and beauty professionals. Our platform enables users to discover salons, book appointments, manage bookings, communicate with salons, and access other related services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use the HeyStyle mobile application, website, or any other services provided by HeyStyle (collectively referred to as the "Platform").

By creating an account, accessing, or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to the collection, use, storage, and disclosure of your information as described in this Policy.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the Platform.

2. Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the HeyStyle Platform, including:

  • Customers who browse, book, or review salon services.
  • Salon owners and authorized representatives who register and manage salon profiles.
  • Salon employees or staff members who access the Platform through a salon account.
  • Business partners who interact with HeyStyle through approved business services.
  • Visitors who browse our website or contact our support team.
  • Any individual who communicates with HeyStyle through email, telephone, social media, or other official communication channels.

This Privacy Policy applies regardless of the device used to access the Platform, including smartphones, tablets, desktop computers, laptops, or web browsers.

3. Information We Collect

To provide and improve our services, we may collect the following categories of information.

Personal Information

When you create an account, book an appointment, register a salon, contact customer support, or otherwise interact with the Platform, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address
  • Profile photograph (optional)
  • Date of birth (optional)
  • Gender (optional)
  • Preferred language
  • City and state
  • Login credentials
  • Unique account identifier

Salon Information

If you register a salon on the Platform, we may collect:

  • Salon name
  • Owner or business representative name
  • Business address
  • Contact details
  • Business email address
  • Business registration information, where applicable
  • GST, PAN, or other tax-related information, where applicable
  • Bank account details when required for payments or settlements
  • Salon logo and photographs
  • Business operating hours
  • Services offered
  • Pricing information
  • Staff details provided by the salon

Appointment Information

When appointments are booked or managed through HeyStyle, we may collect:

  • Appointment date and time
  • Selected services
  • Assigned staff member
  • Booking status
  • Payment status
  • Notes provided by the customer or salon
  • Appointment history

Device Information

To improve performance, maintain security, and troubleshoot technical issues, we may collect:

  • Device model
  • Device manufacturer
  • Operating system version
  • Application version
  • IP address
  • Network information
  • Device language
  • Device identifiers
  • Diagnostic information and crash reports

Location Information

With your permission, HeyStyle may access your device's location to:

  • Display nearby salons
  • Improve search accuracy
  • Support location-based features
  • Help you find services available in your area

You can disable location access at any time through your device settings. Some features may not function as intended if location access is disabled.

Camera and Photo Access

With your permission, HeyStyle may access your device's camera or photo library to allow you to:

  • Upload a profile picture
  • Upload salon images
  • Add portfolio photographs
  • Share images as part of reviews or other supported features

HeyStyle only accesses your camera or photo library after you grant the required permission.

Communications

If you contact us through customer support or other official communication channels, we may retain information such as:

  • Emails
  • Support messages
  • Feedback
  • Complaints
  • Suggestions
  • Other communications necessary to respond to your requests

Payment Information

If payment services are available through the Platform, payment information may be processed by authorized payment service providers. HeyStyle does not intentionally store complete payment card information unless required by applicable law or necessary to provide the requested service.

Automatically Collected Information

When you use the Platform, we may automatically collect information such as:

  • Login times
  • Session duration
  • Features used
  • Pages viewed
  • Search activity within the Platform
  • Application usage statistics
  • Technical performance data

This information helps us improve the Platform, enhance security, and provide a better user experience.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide the Platform: create and manage your account, display salons and services, process and confirm appointments, and enable communication between customers and salons.
  • To personalise your experience: show nearby or relevant salons, remember your preferences, and surface your booking history.
  • To process transactions: facilitate payments, refunds, settlements, and invoices through authorized payment service providers.
  • To communicate with you: send booking confirmations, reminders, status updates, and service or policy notices. These are transactional messages and are necessary to provide the service.
  • To provide customer support: respond to your enquiries, complaints, and feedback, and investigate reported issues.
  • To improve the Platform: understand how features are used, diagnose crashes and technical problems, and develop new features.
  • To maintain safety and security: detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, misuse, unauthorised access, and activity that violates our terms.
  • For marketing, where permitted: send offers, promotions, and updates about HeyStyle. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time without affecting your ability to use the Platform.
  • To comply with law: meet legal, regulatory, tax, and accounting obligations, and respond to lawful requests from authorities.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as described below:

  • With salons you book: when you book an appointment, we share the details the salon needs to serve you — such as your name, contact number, selected services, appointment time, and any notes you add.
  • With service providers: we work with vendors who help us operate the Platform, including cloud hosting, database and storage providers, authentication services, SMS and email delivery providers, push notification services, analytics and crash reporting tools, and payment service providers. They may process your information only on our instructions and for the purposes we specify.
  • For legal reasons: where we believe disclosure is reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, a court order, or a lawful government request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of HeyStyle, our users, or the public.
  • In a business transfer: if HeyStyle is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change in ownership or control of your personal information.
  • With your consent: in any other case, we share your information only when you direct or permit us to.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Your information is stored on secure servers operated by us or by our cloud service providers. Some of these providers may store or process data on servers located outside India. Where information is transferred across borders, we take reasonable steps to ensure it remains protected in a manner consistent with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active or as long as needed to provide the Platform to you. After that, we retain information only where required to comply with legal, tax, accounting, or regulatory obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and abuse, or enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed for these purposes, we delete it or anonymise it.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect your information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, and loss. These include encryption of data in transit, access controls that limit who within HeyStyle can view personal information, and secure authentication for accounts.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential and for notifying us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you have the following rights in relation to your personal information:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: update or correct inaccurate or incomplete information. Most profile details can be edited directly in the app.
  • Deletion: request deletion of your account and associated personal information, as described in the next section.
  • Withdraw consent: revoke permissions such as location, camera, or photo access at any time through your device settings, and withdraw consent for processing that relies on consent.
  • Opt out of marketing: unsubscribe from promotional emails or notifications using the opt-out provided in the message or in your app settings.
  • Grievance redressal: raise a concern about how your information is handled using the contact details below.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at officialheystyle@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your HeyStyle account and the personal information associated with it at any time by emailing officialheystyle@gmail.com from the email address or with the mobile number registered to your account.

Once your request is verified, we will delete your profile information, saved preferences, and uploaded images. Certain records — such as transaction and booking records required for tax, accounting, dispute resolution, or fraud prevention purposes — may be retained for the period required by applicable law, after which they are deleted or anonymised. Information already shared with a salon in connection with a completed appointment remains subject to that salon's own record-keeping.

10. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not intended for children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where a minor requires salon services, the booking should be made by a parent or legal guardian using their own account. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child without appropriate consent, we will take steps to delete it.
