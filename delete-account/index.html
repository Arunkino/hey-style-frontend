Account Deletion

Delete Your HeyStyle for Business Account

This page explains how salon owners and staff using the HeyStyle for Business app (package com.heystyle.app) can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request deletion

To delete your HeyStyle for Business account, send an email requesting deletion from the email address or mobile number registered to your account:

Email officialheystyle@gmail.com

Please include in your email:

  1. The registered mobile number (and email address, if any) linked to your account.
  2. The salon/shop name associated with your account.
  3. A statement that you want your account and data deleted.

We verify the request against the phone number / email on file, then process it. Deletion is completed within 7 business days of a verified request, and we'll confirm by email once it's done.

What is deleted

Once your request is verified, we permanently delete or anonymise:

  • Your account credentials and login details (phone number, email, OTP records)
  • Owner and staff profile details (name, gender, age, bio, specialization)
  • Shop profile information (address, contact details, timings, description)
  • Shop photos and videos uploaded through the app
  • Staff records and shift timings you added
  • Saved device/notification tokens and notification preferences

What is retained, and for how long

Some information cannot be deleted immediately because we're legally required to keep it, or because deleting it would affect other users' records:

  • Booking and appointment records (dates, services, customers served) are retained for as long as required under applicable Indian tax, accounting and consumer-protection law — in most cases up to 7 years — after which they are deleted or anonymised.
  • Transaction/payment status records tied to completed bookings are retained on the same basis, for accounting and dispute-resolution purposes.
  • Information already shared with a customer in connection with a completed appointment (e.g. a booking confirmation) remains on that customer's own account/records.

Everything else is deleted outright; nothing is kept for marketing or any purpose beyond the legal/accounting needs above.

More information

For full details on what we collect and why, see our Privacy Policy. For any questions about this process, contact officialheystyle@gmail.com.
